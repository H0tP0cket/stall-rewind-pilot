Judge behaviour, not the score

Stalled: the agent is not converging and is going round—repeating failed fixes, oscillating, resubmitting unchanged work, chasing unavailable resources, or endlessly checking instead of acting.

Not stalled: it is implementing, narrowing down a cause, or reasonably verifying and finishing.

Unclear: this prefix is insufficient to tell.

A flat score or long stretch alone is not a stall. Do not guess what happens next. Onset is the first step of the ongoing stall, not necessarily the first failure.
